## Why

Dominic asked to apply the "Harness Engineering" article to Agora. Of its 14
principles, Agora already had about 8 in code and the Phase 0–12 work covered
state, caps, grants, recovery, driver outcomes and tests. The gaps were the
contract with `done_when`, evidence as the gate to completion, a verifier
separate from the builder, and a receipt the harness can vouch for. Those are
what this session added. Not added: step-level failure classification and a
per-tool gateway.
